Instructions:

This webpage presents three engravings by the artist Albrecht Duerer:

  • Melencolia I.
  • Knight, Death and Devil.
  • Saint Jerome in his study.

These monochrome engravings have been animated by superimposing coloured details to highlight their content.
The aim is to draw attention to the intricacy of these wonderful examples of Dürer's Symbolist art.
Feel free to move your cursor over the images to see what they reveal.
Some music has been loaded into the player below and is ready to accompany your viewing.
